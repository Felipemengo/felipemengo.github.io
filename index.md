## 🚀 Getting Started
Welcome to **marketing-attribution-shiny**! This app helps you understand your marketing efforts by analyzing how different channels contribute to your overall success. Whether you're interested in multi-touch attribution or just want to see how a small adjustment can impact your ROI, this tool is for you.

## ✔️ Features
- **Multi-Touch Attribution Using Markov Models**: Get a clear view of how different marketing channels interact with each other.
- **Lightweight ROI Simulator**: Test various marketing strategies without spending real money.
- **Interactive Visualizations**: See your data come to life with helpful graphics.
- **Diagnostics and Data Health Checks**: Trust your results with built-in validation tools.
  
## 💻 System Requirements
To use this application, ensure your system meets the following requirements:
- **Operating System**: Windows, macOS, or Linux
- **R Version**: R 4.0 or higher installed
- **Internet Connection**: Required for updates and downloading additional resources

## 🔍 How to Use the App
Using the application is straightforward:

1. **Select Your Data**: Upload your marketing performance data. The app accepts CSV files to make this simple.
2. **Choose an Analysis Type**: Decide if you want to use Markov models for multi-touch attribution or the ROI simulator.
3. **View Results**: After processing, view the output in the interactive dashboard. You can see detailed graphics and explanations to help you make informed decisions.

## 📊 Example Use Cases
